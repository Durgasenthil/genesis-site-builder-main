# Raphael Homoeocare — Website Plan

## Design & Branding

- **Color palette**: Soft sage green, pastel mint, beige, off-white, with gold accents
- **Typography**: Elegant serif headings (Playfair Display) + clean sans-serif body (Poppins) via Google Fonts
- **Style**: Rounded corners, soft shadows, generous white space, calming and professional
- **Animations**: Smooth scroll, subtle fade-in on scroll, gentle hover effects

## Pages & Sections (Single-Page Scrolling Site)

### 1. Sticky Header

    - "Raphael Homoeocare" logo with a leaf/healing icon

- Navigation links: Home, About Homoeopathy, Services, What We Do, FAQ, Contact, Appointment
- Tamil/English language toggle switch (top-right)
- Hamburger menu on mobile

### 2. Hero Section

- Soft gradient background with healing imagery
- Headline: "Gentle Healing. Natural Care. Lasting Wellness."
- Two CTA buttons: Book Appointment + Learn More
- Warm, welcoming visual feel

### 3. About Homoeopathy

- Two-column layout (image + content)
- Explains what homoeopathy is, how it works, safe for all ages
- Icon badges: Natural, Safe, Holistic, No Side Effects

### 4. Our Services (Grid)

- 8 service cards with icons: Chronic Disease, Child Care, Women's Health, Skin Disorders, Allergy & Asthma, Stress & Lifestyle, Hair Fall, General Wellness
- Each card has an icon, short description, and "Learn More" link

### 5. What We Do — Treatment Approach

- Horizontal step-by-step timeline: Consultation → Case Analysis → Personalized Remedy → Follow-Up → Long-term Wellness
- Explanation of patient-centered care approach
- Patient testimonial slider/carousel

### 6. Appointment Form

- Fields: Name, Phone, Email, Age, Service dropdown, Preferred Date & Time, Message, Language Preference
- On submit → redirects to WhatsApp with pre-filled message to your clinic number
- Rounded inputs, validation, mobile-optimized, confirmation feedback

### 7. FAQ (Accordion)

- 6 common questions with smooth expand/collapse animation
- Topics: safety, duration, side effects, children, medical reports, chronic diseases

### 8. Address, Timing & Location

- Split layout: Contact details (address, hours, phone, email) + placeholder Google Map embed
- "Walk-ins welcome | Prior appointment recommended" note

### 9. Footer

- Logo, quick links, contact info, social media icons
- Copyright: "© 2026 Raphael Homoeocare. All Rights Reserved."

## Multilingual (Tamil/English)

- Language toggle in header switches all site content
- Placeholder Tamil text throughout (you replace later with real translations)
- Language preference stored in browser

## Extra Features

- Floating WhatsApp button (bottom-right)
- Sticky "Call Now" bar on mobile
- Smooth scrolling between sections
- Subtle leaf/nature decorative elements
- SEO-friendly markup and meta tags
- Fully responsive, mobile-first design
